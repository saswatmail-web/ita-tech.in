WHO WE ARE

Technology decisions deserve independent thinking.

Independent Technology Advisory (ITA) is a founder-led, vendor-neutral advisory practice based in Pune, India.

We support organisations and technology partners with independent assessment, infrastructure planning, solution review and structured project-delivery support. Our work helps make requirements, options, responsibilities, risks and milestones clear—so clients can make informed decisions and maintain visibility through delivery.

ITA provides professional advisory and oversight services alongside clients and their appointed system integrators, OEMs and contractors. Hardware supply, installation and implementation remain with the contracted delivery partner unless a separate written scope is agreed.

HOW WE WORK

From first assessment to clear handover.

Every engagement begins with agreed objectives, scope, deliverables and decision rights. Recommendations are documented, assumptions are visible and delivery is tracked against agreed milestones.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Assess

Understand the operating environment, objectives, requirements, constraints and current state.

02 / DEFINE

Plan

Document options, assumptions, priorities, scope, responsibilities and the recommended path forward.

03 / ALIGN

Coordinate

Bring stakeholders and delivery partners together around actions, dependencies, risks and milestones.

04 / REVIEW

Review

Review progress against agreed scope and quality expectations, making risks and open items visible.

05 / HANDOVER

Handover

Record outcomes, outstanding items, decisions and the documentation needed for an orderly close.
