Technology decisions deserve independent thinking.

Independent Technology Advisory (ITA) is a founder-led consulting practice focused on enterprise IT and data infrastructure.

We help organisations and technology partners bring structure to infrastructure decisions, assess existing environments, review proposed solutions and maintain visibility across complex delivery programmes.

Our role is to provide clear advice, practical documentation and independent oversight—complementing implementation partners without competing to supply hardware or installation services.
